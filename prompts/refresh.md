# Refresh prompt

Use at the start of a campaign cycle, each quarter, or after a big change in wins, losses, competitors or proof points. Replace the path.

```
Use the product-marketing-agent. Data folder: /path/to/your/private-data. Today's date: YYYY-MM-DD.
Rebuild the positioning and check it. Tell me: the ICP status and segments that need my decision, where the target list disagrees with our wins, which proof points are expired or expiring, what I must supply, and what changed compared with the last version I have.
Do not record any decision or approval for me. Do not publish or send anything.
```

Keep your data folder private. Do not commit it to the public repo.
