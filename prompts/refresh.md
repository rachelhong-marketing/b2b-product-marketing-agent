# Refresh prompt

Use at the start of a campaign cycle, each quarter, or after a big change in wins, losses or competitors. Replace the path.

```
Use the product-marketing-agent. Data folder: /path/to/your/private-data.
Rebuild the positioning, check it, and tell me: best-fit segments, claims without proof, what I must supply, and what changed in the segments or pillars compared with the last version I have.
Do not publish or send anything.
```

Keep your data folder private. Do not commit it to the public repo.
