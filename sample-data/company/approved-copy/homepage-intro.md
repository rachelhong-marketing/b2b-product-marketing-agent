# Made-up homepage intro

Your customer data should tell one story. Fernhill Data Quality Platform finds duplicate records across your systems, merges them without breaking your reports, and keeps watching so they do not come back.

Most teams treat duplicate records as a one-time cleanup. Then the same mess is back within months. We built the platform for the second part of the job: keeping data clean after the cleanup.

Setup takes under two weeks. You see how many duplicate records you have within a day of connecting a system. A person approves every merge, so you stay in control of what changes.
