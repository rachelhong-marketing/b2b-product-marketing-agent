# Made-up case study summary

A regional bank had customer records spread across three systems. Month-end reports never matched, and each team trusted a different number.

The team connected the platform, reviewed the duplicates it found, and merged them in stages. Month-end reports did not change. Because every merge was approved by a person, nobody had to worry about losing customer history.

Now the platform watches for new duplicates, and the team reviews them once a week instead of running a cleanup project every year.
