# Brand voice guide (draft): Fernhill Software

Prepared as of 2026-11-24. These are patterns found in copy that is already approved. The product marketer decides which of them are the voice.

## Your own words

- Voice note in the product profile: Plain, confident, practical. Talk like a smart colleague, not a billboard.

## Observed patterns to consider as rules

- Sentence length: approved copy averages 13 words. Short and direct.
- Point of view: addresses the reader directly more than it talks about the company.
- Tone markers: no exclamation marks.
