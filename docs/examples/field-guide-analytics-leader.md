## What to lead with

- Find them: see how many duplicate records you have across systems (answers 8 interview pains)
- Keep them out: monitoring so duplicate records don't come back (answers 4 interview pains)
- Merge them: one record per customer, without breaking reports (answers 3 interview pains)
