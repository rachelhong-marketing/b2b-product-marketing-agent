# Customer survey: question set

Prepared as of 2026-11-24. Eight questions. Options come from the pains, alternatives, roles and segments already in your data. Keep it short, offer an anonymous option, and do not send the same person more than two surveys a year. Treat a handful of replies as stories, not statistics.

| # | Question | Type | Options |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which best describes your role? | choose one | BI Director; CFO; Director of Analytics; Head of Data Engineering; VP Finance; VP of Data; Other (please say) |
| 2 | How many employees does your company have? | choose one | 1000-10000; over 10000; under 1000 |
| 3 | Which industry are you in? | choose one | Financial services; Healthcare; Manufacturing; Retail; Technology; Other (please say) |
| 4 | Which of these problems matters most to you right now? | choose one | duplicates come back after cleanup; reports do not match; merging will break reports; which record is the right one; Other (please say) |
| 5 | How do you handle it today? | choose one | a one-time cleanup project; spreadsheet reconciliation; do it ourselves this quarter; manual rules in scripts; Other (please say) |
| 6 | How often does it cause a problem? | choose one | Every week; Every month; Every quarter; Rarely or never |
| 7 | What is the biggest obstacle to fixing it? | open answer | - |
| 8 | May we contact you for a short follow-up conversation? | choose one | Yes; No |
