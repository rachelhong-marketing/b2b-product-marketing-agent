## Messaging pillars

Chosen from your claimed differentiators: no tracked competitor makes the claim (40%), customers talk about the pain it answers (35%), proof on file (25%).

### Pillar 1: Keep them out: monitoring so duplicate records don't come back

- Score 100.0. No tracked competitor makes this claim.
- Pain it answers: duplicates come back after cleanup. Mentioned in 11 of 30 interviews.
- Proof (customer survey, source: 2025 customer survey, n=120): Teams spend 30% of their week fixing data
- Proof (customer survey, source: 2025 customer survey, n=120): Customers cut data cleanup time by 40%

### Pillar 2: Merge them: one record per customer, without breaking reports

- Score 100.0. No tracked competitor makes this claim.
- Pain it answers: merging will break reports, which record is the right one. Mentioned in 11 of 30 interviews.
- Proof (customer survey, source: 2025 customer survey, n=120): Customers cut data cleanup time by 40%
- Proof (customer result, source: made-up customer webinar): Made-up: a regional bank merged its customer records and month-end reports did not change

### Pillar 3: Find them: see how many duplicate records you have across systems

- Score 93.0. No tracked competitor makes this claim.
- Pain it answers: reports do not match. Mentioned in 8 of 30 interviews.
- Proof (benchmark, source: made-up implementation note): Made-up: duplicate count shown within one day of connecting a system
- Proof (benchmark, source: made-up pilot summary): Made-up: the first scan found duplicates in all 20 pilot accounts
